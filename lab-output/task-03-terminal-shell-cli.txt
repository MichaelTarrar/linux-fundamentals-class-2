
===== Step 3.1: Identify your terminal and shell =====
ubuntu@vm:~$ tty
/dev/pts/1
ubuntu@vm:~$ echo $0
-bash
ubuntu@vm:~$ echo $SHELL
/bin/bash
ubuntu@vm:~$ ps -p $$
  PID TTY          TIME CMD
 6642 pts/1    00:00:00 bash

===== Step 3.2: List the shells installed on the system =====
ubuntu@vm:~$ cat /etc/shells
# /etc/shells: valid login shells
/bin/sh
/usr/bin/sh
/bin/bash
/usr/bin/bash
/bin/rbash
/usr/bin/rbash
/usr/bin/dash
/usr/bin/tmux

===== Step 3.3: Prepare files, then run pwd, ls and whoami =====
ubuntu@vm:~$ mkdir Documents projects
ubuntu@vm:~$ touch notes.txt
ubuntu@vm:~$ pwd
/home/ubuntu
ubuntu@vm:~$ ls
Documents  notes.txt  projects
ubuntu@vm:~$ whoami
ubuntu

===== Step 3.4: Break a command into its three parts =====
ubuntu@vm:~$ ls -l /home
total 4
drwxr-x--- 4 ubuntu ubuntu 4096 Nov 21 20:32 ubuntu
